## Editing / 编辑

Open a downloaded `.drawio.svg` in draw.io to recover the embedded native diagram.
Edit the shapes and connectors, then export SVG and PNG with **Include a copy of
my diagram** enabled. Use a light theme and a 2× scale for the PNG. Keep the English
and Chinese variants aligned in meaning. Temporary `.drawio` files are not needed
after the editable exports are verified.

用 draw.io 打开下载的 `.drawio.svg`，即可恢复内嵌图稿。编辑形状与连线，导出时启用
“包含图表副本”，生成浅色 SVG 和双倍尺寸 PNG。中英文修改保持语义对应；确认可编辑导出
完整后，不需要保留临时 `.drawio` 文件。

diagram roles and do not certify scientific acceptance.
